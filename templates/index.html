System Workflow & Architecture

1

Image Ingestion & Validation

Uploads are validated for allowed format (JPG/PNG/WEBP), maximum size (10 MB), and decoded via OpenCV to prevent corrupt input.

2

Pretrained YOLO11n Inference

Ultralytics YOLO11 nano model detects 80 COCO dataset classes in a single forward pass with high frame rate and low latency.

3

OpenCV Visualization

High-contrast color-coded bounding boxes, class labels, and percentage confidence badges are rendered onto the image.

4

Analytics & Export

Extracts class-wise counts, bounding box coordinates, benchmark processing time, and provides high-resolution download.
